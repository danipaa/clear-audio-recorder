Privacy Policy

Clear Audio Recorder · last updated 10 November 2026

Your recordings stay on your phone

Clear Audio Recorder records, cleans and edits audio entirely on your device. Your recordings are never uploaded to us and we cannot access them. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes them. "Send to PC" moves files directly between your phone and your computer over your own Wi-Fi; nothing passes through our servers.

Microphone

The microphone is used only while you are recording.

Optional account (Google sign-in)

You can use the app without an account. If you sign in with Google, we store: your name, email address and profile photo link from Google; your plan (free or Pro); how many free cleaning seconds you have used; the names of your folders; your app settings; and simple usage counts (for example how many recordings you cleaned or exported each day). We use this to restore your plan and settings on a new phone, to prevent abuse of the free allowance, and to understand how the app is used. This data is stored with Google Firebase.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, and diagnostic data to show and measure ads. See Google's privacy policy. Users in the EEA and UK are asked for consent first. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Subscriptions

Payments are handled by Google Play. We use RevenueCat to confirm whether your subscription is active. We never see your card details.

Deleting your data

In the app, open You → Delete account. This permanently removes your profile and usage history from our database. You can also email us at [your support email] from the address you signed in with and we will delete it within 30 days. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in Google Play → Subscriptions.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. It is shared only with the service providers named above (Google Firebase, Google AdMob, Google Play, RevenueCat) so the app can work.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
